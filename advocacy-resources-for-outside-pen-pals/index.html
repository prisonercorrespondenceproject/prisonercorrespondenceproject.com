Advocacy Resources For Outside Pen Pals

One of PCP’s goals as a prisoner solidarity project is to build our collective capacity to fight for prisoners on an individual and structural level. We want to empower outside pen pals to advocate for comrades in prison.

If your pen pal is facing abuse or needs support from the outside, but you don’t know where to start, please get in touch with us!
