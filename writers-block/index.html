Writer’s Block

WRITER’S BLOCK is our monthly penpal co-working session, where penpals get together to write/type their letters, meet other penpals, support each other, and add letter-writing to our calendars so it doesn’t fall to the wayside.

We’ve often heard from outside penpals that they struggle to find time to sit down and write letters. These sessions were created specifically to address that need! A member of the PCP Collective is always there to facilitate, and will be happy to answer questions about penpal-ing.

You have to register in advance for Writer’s Block using the link below. We will send you a reminder email before the session, and let you know when next sessions are planned.

We meet on the fourth Wednesday of every month, from 5-6pm PT / 8-9pm ET
