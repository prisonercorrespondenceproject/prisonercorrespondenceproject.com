Find a Penpal

After signing in below, you will see the self-descriptions of everybody who's currently waiting for a penpal. Whenever someone gets matched up, their profile gets taken down from this page.

At the top right there's a drop-down menu to help you target your search. Keep in mind that our inside membership largely reflects the demographics of who is in prison (upwards of 90% of incarcerated people are in men's prisons, and the United States has a much higher prison population). The Search function allows you to filter by geographic location, and to search for keywords (such as hobbies or interests) in members' descriptions. Give it a try! It will help you to narrow down your options and find a good fit.

The "When They Got Involved" field indicates when someone first made contact with the Project, not necessarily how long they've been waiting for a penpal: some just wanted to be on the mailing list in the beginning, some had penpals who'd stopped writing and are now waiting to be rematched, etc.

That being said, the ones closer to the top have generally been waiting the longest, so if you're trying to choose between a few people, we encourage you to pick someone closer to the top of the list.

Please be aware that these bios were all written by our inside members themselves and do not necessarily reflect the views of the organization.

Let us know who you want to correspond with and we'll send you the address. Get in touch if you have any questions!
